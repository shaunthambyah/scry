Inference scaling and reasoning

Researchers tested whether allocating more inference compute improves reasoning while keeping model weights fixed.

Scaling results

Increasing inference time improved accuracy on several multi-step reasoning tasks in the evaluated models.

The gains depended on the task and diminished after the search budget passed a useful range.

Limitations

These results do not establish that every model benefits equally from additional inference compute.
  • All comparisons used the same model weights.
  • Evaluation covered a limited set of reasoning benchmarks.
